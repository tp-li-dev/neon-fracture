NEON FRACTURE PWA bundle

Upload these files to the repository root (main branch):
- index.html
- manifest.webmanifest
- sw.js
- icon-192.png
- icon-512.png

GitHub Pages:
https://tp-li-dev.github.io/neon-fracture/

On iPhone/iPad:
Safari -> Share -> Add to Home Screen
Launch from the Home Screen for standalone/PWA display.

Notes:
- Tailwind remains CDN-based as requested.
- The service worker caches the Tailwind CDN response after the first successful online load.
- Mobile gameplay uses floating sticks: touch/hold or drag in the lower-left/lower-right control zones.
